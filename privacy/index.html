Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026

PupNote requires no account. Your dog profile, health records, routines, observations, medication information, weight entries, notes and photos are stored locally on your device. PupNote uses privacy-preserving TelemetryDeck analytics only to understand general feature use and improve reliability; your dog-health content is not included.

Information you enter

PupNote lets you enter a dog profile, routines, observations, medication and vaccination information, health history, weight, notes and related records. This information is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The developer cannot access it remotely and does not sell, rent or use it for advertising or tracking.

Photos, reports, exports and notifications

If you choose a profile photo, PupNote stores the selected image with the app’s local data. PDF reports and readable data exports are created on your device and leave the app only when you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. An exported file is a readable copy and is not a restorable PupNote backup.

Medication reminders are scheduled locally through Apple’s notification system using times you enter. PupNote does not send medication names, instructions or reminder content to the developer or TelemetryDeck.

Local storage and device backups

PupNote does not operate a separate cloud-sync or backup service in this release. If iPhone Backup or a computer backup is enabled, iOS may include PupNote’s local app data in that device backup according to Apple’s settings and policies. Deleting the app may delete its locally stored records.

Anonymous analytics through TelemetryDeck

If you choose to allow analytics, PupNote uses TelemetryDeck to understand how app features are used and to improve reliability. Analytics may include an anonymized installation identifier, actions such as opening a screen or using a feature, a timestamp rounded to the nearest hour, and technical information such as the PupNote version, iOS version, build number and device model.

PupNote does not send TelemetryDeck your dog’s profile, health records, symptoms, diagnoses, medication names or instructions, weights, notes, photos, report or export contents, email address, or other user-entered text. Events and parameters are limited to predefined technical and feature-use values.

The analytics are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. TelemetryDeck states that it anonymizes identifiers before transmission and does not store IP addresses. TelemetryDeck processes this information under privacy protections that are intended to meet or exceed those described here. Read TelemetryDeck’s privacy information.

You can decline analytics without losing PupNote’s core functionality. You can also change your choice in PupNote’s settings. Analytics already received cannot be connected back to you or your dog for individual retrieval or deletion. Analytics remain available according to the retention period of the developer’s TelemetryDeck plan and TelemetryDeck’s account and retention policies.

Data retention and deletion

Your PupNote records remain on your device until you remove them. Use Profile → Settings → Erase All Data to delete the dog profile, logs, records, local preferences and pending medication reminders. Deleting the app also removes local app data according to iOS behavior.

Because the developer does not receive your locally stored dog-health information, the developer cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

When you contact support

You decide what information to include when contacting support. Support emails are used only to respond to your request and are deleted from the developer’s mailbox when no longer reasonably needed. Do not send dog-health records, private notes, photos, PDF reports or exported PupNote files.

Privacy questions and rights

If you have a privacy question or request concerning support correspondence, contact itotoro525@gmail.com. Where applicable, you also have the right to contact your local data-protection authority.

Children

PupNote is a general dog-care record tool. It is not directed to children and is not intended for the App Store Kids Category. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Optional anonymous analytics are processed only after the user allows them, as described above.

Veterinary notice

PupNote organizes information you choose to enter. It does not diagnose, assess emergencies, recommend treatment or replace professional veterinary advice. Contact a veterinarian whenever you are concerned about your dog’s health. For an urgent or potentially life-threatening situation, contact an emergency veterinarian immediately.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app, website or data practices change. Material changes will be communicated through the app, this website or the App Store listing before they take effect where reasonably possible. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy or support can be sent to itotoro525@gmail.com. You can also visit the PupNote support page.
