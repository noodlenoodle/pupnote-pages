Why PupNote

Different vets.
One continuous story.

Your dog may meet different veterinarians and visit different clinics throughout life. PupNote gives you one continuous health journal that travels with you.

Record small changes in appetite, energy, mobility, behaviour and daily routines while the details are still fresh. When it is time for a veterinary visit, turn those observations, medications and health records into a clear timeline and organized summary.

PupNote does not diagnose. It helps you remember what happened, when it happened and what changed—so you can give the veterinarian clearer context.
